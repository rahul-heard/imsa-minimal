What does a music supervisor do?

Music supervision is the process of shaping, sourcing and managing music for film, television, advertising, gaming and other screen-based storytelling. Music supervisors work where creative, commercial and rights-related decisions meet.

The work can include

  • Finding and curating music
  • Choosing songs and where they go
  • Licensing and clearing compositions and master recordings
  • Working with artists, labels, publishers and composers
  • Collaborating with directors, producers and editors
  • Budgeting, scheduling, cue sheets and delivery

Every project is different. A supervisor may take on some of this work, not all of it.
